# Play Empire of Gods

Open **empire.html** for Business Command, or **index.html** for the animated Olympus Station demo.

The hosted game is static. It does not connect to local services, run real workflows, send messages, or spend money. Campaign credits, XP, days, missions, and rewards are fictional. Real revenue remains **Not connected**.

1. Choose **Web Studio**, then **Build practice blueprint**.
2. Complete its trial and follow the prerequisite links to unlock other categories.
3. Use **Next practice day** when you need more focus.
4. Complete all eighteen missions across nine categories to finish the campaign.

A focused trial costs two focus; a crafted trial costs three and earns an extra fifteen fictional credits and twenty XP. Completed missions cannot award rewards twice.

Progress is saved in your current browser when storage is available. **Export save** downloads a backup; **Import save** restores a validated backup. Saves do not sync between devices, browsers, or website addresses. Export before changing addresses or clearing browser data. If storage is unavailable, the game warns that progress is session-only. **Reset** asks before replacing progress.

The **Operations** tab on the hosted website offers planning briefs only. Its local-state refresh is disabled, and Content Security Policy blocks network connections. Planning briefs are drafts and do not authorize real work.

The optional developer bridge is documented in the repository's bridge guide. It is not hosted by GitHub Pages and is not needed to play.
